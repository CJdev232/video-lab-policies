Privacy Policy for video-lab

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Introduction

video-lab ("we", "us", or "our") is a personal development tool used to generate and publish short AI-created videos to platforms like TikTok. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when using the TikTok Content Posting API integration.

Information We Collect

This tool does not collect personal information from end users. It is designed for the account owner to post their own video content.

  • Video files you choose to upload (processed locally and sent only to TikTok via the official API)
  • Authorization tokens obtained through TikTok OAuth (stored locally on your machine in ~/.config/ai-video-lab/tokens/)

How We Use Information

We use the TikTok OAuth flow solely to authenticate and upload videos on your behalf to your own TikTok account. No data is shared with third parties.

Data Storage

All tokens and configuration are stored locally on your device. No cloud storage or external databases are used by this tool.

Third-Party Services

This tool uses the official TikTok Content Posting API and OAuth services. Please refer to TikTok's Developer Terms and privacy policy for how TikTok handles data.

Your Choices

You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings under "Apps and websites". Local tokens can be deleted from the tokens folder.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer directly.

This is a personal/internal tool and not a commercial product.
